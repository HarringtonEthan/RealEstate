      How this works

      A research assistant program reads public posts (like Reddit) and public county records looking for people in the Triangle who have clearly said they want to buy, sell, relocate, or invest. It throws away everything vague or old, researches the rest, and gives each opportunity a score out of 100 with a written explanation.

      Nothing here is contacted automatically — ever. Every card is just information for you to review. Before reaching out to anyone, open the source link, read it yourself, and use your own judgment (and the community’s etiquette — a helpful reply beats a pitch every time).

      All information comes from public sources and is provided for research only. Leads are never selected, scored, or filtered based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or any other protected characteristic. Verify everything independently before acting on it.
