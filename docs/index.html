      How this works

      A research assistant program reads public posts (like Reddit) and public county records looking for people in the Triangle who have clearly said they want to buy, sell, relocate, or invest. It throws away everything vague or old, researches the rest, and gives each opportunity a score out of 100 with a written explanation.

      Nothing here is contacted automatically — ever. Every card links to the original public post so you can read it yourself and decide. For forum leads, the post is the contact path — a genuinely helpful reply beats a pitch every time. The system only ever shows contact details a person published themselves.
